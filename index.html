Before you begin
Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Allow microphone access. For live microphone captions, open this app through HTTPS or localhost.
Audio & privacy: Select the church mixer/interface as the laptop's default input in Windows/macOS sound settings before starting. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider for processing; review its privacy terms. This app does not store raw audio.

Microphone tip: For clearer church captions, take a clean AUX/REC/USB feed from the mixer into the laptop audio interface. Do not connect an amplifier speaker output directly to the laptop.
